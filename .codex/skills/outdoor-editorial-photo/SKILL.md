# Outdoor Editorial Photo

Use this skill for photo edits intended for the VanVenture website.

## Preserve the documentary original

- Edit an existing photo; never regenerate its scene.
- Preserve people, faces, body proportions, expression, clothing, equipment, vehicles, animals, landscape, and composition exactly.
- Do not add, move, replace, crop, reshape, or remove pictured elements, except for approved privacy work or explicitly approved distraction removal.
- Treat every AI-assisted result as a review derivative. Compare it visually with the unchanged project copy before publication. Do not use it as the sole retained source.

## Privacy and source handling

- Archive originals are read-only. First retain an unchanged project copy and record the source path and checksum when an archive original is used.
- Anonymize every visible vehicle license plate in a web derivative with a subtle, professional blur or pixelation.
- Do not publish recognizable children unless the user explicitly selects the photo.

## Distraction removal requires confirmation

- Identify human-made distractions such as power lines, poles, fences, bins, or barriers.
- Do not remove them until the user explicitly confirms the exact elements. Keep them unchanged in the first grading pass.

## Print-magazine grade

- Use natural, earthy color. Keep greens moody but plausible; slightly desaturate sky/water blues; subtly support warm browns and oranges.
- Apply controlled filmic contrast and a gently raised, matte black point. Preserve realistic highlight and shadow detail.
- Preserve real skin texture and midday/daylight character. Avoid beauty retouching, HDR, artificial bokeh, dramatic relighting, oversaturation, or invented golden-hour light.

## Delivery

- Save derivatives alongside the relevant project assets without overwriting the unchanged source.
- Before publishing, confirm the visual result respects the original and that all plates are anonymized.
